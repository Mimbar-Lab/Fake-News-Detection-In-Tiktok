# Specialized Technology

## Chosen technology
Machine learning (supervised, multimodal classification)

## Why this technology
My problem is that TikTok viewers cannot easily tell whether a news video is fake, and fact-checkers cannot check videos as fast as they spread. A supervised machine learning model can learn from labelled fake and real videos and then score a new video in seconds, so a person can check the most suspicious videos first.
